Chapter One · Work

Academic teaching, translation practice, and editorial judgment.

From translator to lecturer to founder.

The foundation · 2016 - 2022

Academic and Legal Translator · UMM Language Center & Palawa Karya

High-stakes academic and legal translation shaped the professional base later carried into translation studies research, graduate linguistics training, and scholarly editing.

The classroom · 2022 - Present

Lecturer · Universitas Terbuka

Teaching and research in English language and literature, with classroom work connected to publishable inquiry in sociolinguistics, discourse analysis, translation, and English for Specific Purposes.

The practice · Founder

TranSaintika · Language Services

A Malang-based practice built around academically grounded translation judgment: terminology control, genre sensitivity, discourse-level equivalence, and context-aware Indonesian-English transfer.

My core work sits where texts meet institutions: how meaning travels across language, genre, ideology, classroom practice, and publication standards.
